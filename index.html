TTM Guide

Getting Started with TTM

Through The Mail (TTM) autograph collecting is a rewarding hobby where you send cards and letters to athletes, celebrities, and public figures requesting autographs.

Basic Steps:

  1. Review signer information shared by the collecting community
  2. Prepare your card and a polite letter
  3. Check current postage for your outgoing mail and any return envelope
  4. Address your envelope neatly and clearly
  5. Mail and wait patiently (response times vary)

Pro Tips:

  • Use the directory to review community-reported details and verify information independently
  • Include a self-addressed stamped envelope (SASE) for better results
  • Be patient - some athletes take months to respond
  • Track your requests in our dashboard

Best Practices

Maximize Your Success Rate

📝 Write Polite Letters

Be respectful, concise, and genuine. Athletes appreciate thoughtful requests.

🎯 Target Active Athletes

Focus on currently active players. Retired athletes may have different response patterns.

⏰ Time Your Requests

Avoid during peak seasons (playoffs, holidays) when athletes are busiest.

📦 Use Quality Materials

Send cards in topload holders with cardboard protection. This shows respect for their signature.

🔄 Track Your Results

Use our dashboard to monitor success rates and share results with the community.
