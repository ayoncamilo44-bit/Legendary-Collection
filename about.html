Built for the through-the-mail collector.

Legitimate Collector brings signer research, collecting logs, and shared TTM experiences together in one independent community resource.

What you can do here

  • Explore the signer directory and review community-maintained TTM details.
  • Keep track of signer records and import your own CSV data after signing in.
  • Share a collecting update and learn from other collectors' reported experiences.
  • Suggest a signer entry for the shared directory.

Independent and community-maintained

Legitimate Collector is an independent fan project, not an official site of any athlete, team, league, card maker, or autograph service. Directory entries and response reports are community supplied, can become outdated, and are not guarantees of a reply or autograph.

When contributing, use appropriate public or business mailing destinations only. Do not publish private home addresses, personal contact details, or information you do not have permission to share.
