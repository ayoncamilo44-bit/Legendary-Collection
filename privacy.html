Privacy notice

Last updated October 3, 2026. This notice describes the information handled when you browse or contribute to Legitimate Collector.

Information the site handles

  • Google sign-in: Supabase Auth uses Google OAuth. The site receives account identity details needed to sign you in, such as your name and email address. Supabase processes account and session data for authentication, and the sign-in session is kept in your browser so the site can recognize you.
  • Community posts: if you publish a post, its display name, player name, result, optional response time, message, account identifier, and posting time are stored in Supabase and visible publicly.
  • Signer contributions: entries and imported CSV records are stored in the public signer directory. Submitted names, sports, teams, reported outcomes, and mailing details may be visible to anyone. Submit only information appropriate for public sharing; never submit private home addresses or personal contact details.
  • CSV imports: a chosen file is read in your browser and the supported signer fields are sent to Supabase when you submit an import. Do not include unrelated or sensitive information in the file.
  • Hosting and external services: GitHub Pages hosts the site. Google Fonts, Lucide, Tailwind CDN, Supabase, and linked services may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser connects to them.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is being prepared but is not active until the site owner configures its Measurement ID. If enabled, the site will ask first and keep analytics disabled unless you choose to allow it. After you opt in, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage; you can reject or change it through Cookie settings in the footer. Analytics events are limited to page views, Google sign-in clicks, directory search activity without the search text, and affiliate-link clicks without the searched player name.

Affiliate links

Some eBay and Sportlots links are affiliate links. A partner may receive information when you follow its link, and a purchase may earn the site a commission at no extra cost to you. The partner's own privacy notice applies after you leave this site.

Public content and retention

Directory entries and community posts are public. Do not publish information that should remain private. Data is kept in the connected Supabase project until the site owner or service removes it; this static site does not currently provide self-service deletion.

Children

This community site is intended for general sports collectors and is not designed to collect information directly from children. A parent or guardian should supervise any use by a minor.

Contact and privacy requests

A dedicated private contact email is not configured yet. Please do not send personal or account information through public community posts or GitHub issues. See the Contact page for current contact options.

For privacy questions or requests, email legendary1collection@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or Google sign-in codes.

This notice is a practical description of the current website, not legal advice. It should be reviewed for the laws that apply to the site owner and visitors.
